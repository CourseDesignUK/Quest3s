Section 01 — Complete System Architecture (unit.glb)

Integrated Pneumatic & Electronic Architecture

The complete Dräger PSS® 7000 assembly unifies a carbon-composite carrying system, high-pressure compressed air cylinder, positive-pressure breathing loop, and the Bodyguard® 7000 electronic monitoring unit into a single streamlined profile.

Recessed Hose & Cable Protection

Medium-pressure air supply hoses and Bodyguard® 7000 telemetry cables are routed directly through protective channels moulded into the carbon backplate, drastically reducing the risk of snagging in structural collapse environments.

Modular Quick-Release Subsystems

The shoulder harness, waist pad, pneumatics, and cylinder cam-lock strap detach without tools—allowing rapid post-incident decontamination and turnaround on the fireground.

Section 02 — Carrying System & Reducer (body.glb)

Carbon-Composite Backplate & Dynamic Harness

Isolated in body.glb, the carrying frame is moulded from high-strength, anti-static carbon-fiber composite designed to transfer the load onto the wearer’s hips and pelvic center of gravity.

3-Position Torso Height Adjustment

Dual spring-loaded stainless-steel slides allow the shoulder yoke to lock securely into Short (S), Medium (M), or Long (L) positions in seconds, even while wearing firefighting gloves.

Pivoting & Sliding Waist Pad

Mounted on a low-friction stainless-steel pivot and vertical slide mechanism, the waist belt articulates independently as the firefighter bends, twists, climbs ladders, or crawls.

Vulcanized Chloroprene & Kevlar® Harness

Compression-moulded vulcanized chloroprene rubber padding with flame-retardant Kevlar® aramid webbing and high-visibility reflective trim resists chemical absorption and extreme radiant heat.

Balanced Piston Pressure Reducer & 55 bar Whistle

Steps 300 bar cylinder pressure down to a stable 6.5 – 8.0 bar medium pressure (relief valve opens at >11 bar) and triggers a >90 dBA pneumatic warning whistle at 55 ± 5 bar.

Section 04 — Facepiece, LDV & HUD (mask.glb)

Dräger FPS® 7000 Mask & Plus-Pressure LDV

Displayed in mask.glb, the FPS® 7000 full-face mask maintains a continuous positive pressure of 3.0 – 3.5 mbar inside the breathing cavity so toxic smoke cannot leak inward.

Plus-Pressure Lung Demand Valve (LDV)

Engages automatically on the wearer’s first breath, supplying over 500 L/min peak airflow. Includes a front reset button to shut off positive pressure when doffing and a red bypass override button for supplementary air.

Class 1 Polycarbonate Visor & Double-Reflex Seal

Wide-angle distortion-free polycarbonate visor with anti-fog/anti-scratch coating, hypoallergenic EPDM or silicone double-reflex facial seal, and stainless-steel speech diaphragm.
