Cast your screen to a laptop

The headset and laptop must be on the same Wi-Fi network, and you must be signed in to the same Meta account on both.

    Casting won’t connect?

    Use Chrome or Edge, switch off any VPN, and make sure the laptop isn’t on a guest or hotel network that blocks device-to-device traffic. For a wired option, Meta Quest Developer Hub can mirror over a USB-C cable.

    Change the Wi-Fi network

    Do this inside the headset. If it can’t reach any network, set up Wi-Fi from the Meta Horizon phone app instead.

      Soft reset a frozen headset

      A soft reset restarts the headset and keeps your games, accounts and settings.

        Practice the hold

        Don’t confuse this with a factory reset

        Holding power and volume down together opens the boot menu. Choosing “Factory reset” there erases everything on the headset. Only use it if a soft reset doesn’t fix the problem.
